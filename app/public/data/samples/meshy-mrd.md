# MRD: Meshy B2B acquisition, SMB first

*Working document for the Meshy Demand Gen take-home. Facts come from Meshy's public pages, a review readout of 1,828 Trustpilot and G2 reviews (Nov 11, 2026), and a messaging teardown (Nov 9, 2026). Personas are illustrative composites.*

**Owner:** Demand generation · **Horizon:** first 90 days

## Market

Meshy has a strong self-serve base and wants net-new business customers, starting with SMBs. Four segments buy, and they buy differently. We weight them equally in testing because we don't yet know which one converts best at SMB economics.

1. **Amateurs.** Makers, hobby game builders and adult student creators. Buy alone on a card.
2. **Individual pros and startups.** Solo commercial devs, freelance 3D artists, agent builders, product-visual startups and print or e-commerce shops. Buy on a card, read pricing closely.
3. **SMB game studios (10 to 200 people).** Bottom-up adoption by artists, top-down sign-off by the studio head. The near-term B2B focus.
4. **Large studios, publishers and art outsourcers (200+).** Buy on risk. Legal and InfoSec can stall a deal for months.

## Buying group

- **Studio head (economic buyer).** Wants lower cost per asset and an AI-use answer for the publisher.
- **Art director (champion).** Owns the pipeline and the art bar. Fears cleanup work.
- **Environment or prop artist (quiet veto).** Fears for authorship and the job. Decides whether a pilot survives.
- **Tech artist (technical evaluator).** Poly budgets, export formats, engine-safe textures.
- **Producer (schedule owner).** Milestones and vendor spend.
- **Legal, InfoSec, procurement (vetoes at large studios).** Provenance, indemnity, audit reports, standard paper.
- **Solo buyers.** Price, credits, commercial rights and whether the free tier ends in a usable file.

## Competitive landscape

- **Tripo AI.** Claims production-ready quad topology and a bigger user base (20M creators). Closest rival; raised about $400M in 2026.
- **Hyper3D Rodin.** Claims the highest quality and control. Puts enterprise features on its homepage. Also claims licensed training data.
- **Tencent Hunyuan3D.** Free and open weights. Studios can self-host it. Regional license limits and Tencent ownership are procurement concerns for some Western studios.
- **Google Antigravity.** Agents build simple 3D games from code and primitives. Threat at the indie end, not for anything judged as an asset.
- **Outsourcing and doing nothing.** The real alternative in most studio deals.

Meshy's open position: the AI 3D platform studios can defend to their artists and their lawyers. Neither Tripo nor Rodin claims it.

## Product capabilities in scope

- **Image and text to 3D.** Textured models from a sketch, photo or prompt in about a minute. Built for amateurs, solo devs and freelancers.
- **Rigging and animation.** Auto-rigging and 600+ animations in the same tool. Built for solo devs and art directors.
- **Print-ready export.** Watertight, slicer-ready models sent straight to Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer. Built for makers and print shops.
- **Engine and DCC plugins.** Send models into Blender, Unity, Unreal, Godot, Maya and Roblox Studio. Built for tech artists and solo devs.
- **API and MCP for agents.** Generate and refine from Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or a custom pipeline. Built for agent builders and studio CTOs.
- **Studio workspace.** Shared seats, shared credits, private by default, with an admin switch to opt out of model training. Built for art directors and studio heads.
- **Enterprise controls.** SAML SSO, SOC 2 and ISO 27001, customer-set retention, training exclusion by contract. Built for legal, InfoSec and procurement.
- **Meshy Certified program (proposed).** A free 60 to 90 minute course and practical exam for 3D artists, tech artists and print specialists. Agencies and print shops with two certified staff become Certified Partners. Built for artists and shop owners.

## Pricing

Free (100 credits a month), Pro $20 a month, Studio $60 per seat per month ($576 a year billed annually), Enterprise custom. A 2-seat Studio account is about $120 a month, so a 6-month payback caps acquisition cost near $700 before gross margin.

## What the reviews say

- Wins: speed (50% of 4+ star G2 likes), ease for non-modelers (35%), game workflow (25%), print pipeline, rising API and agent praise.
- Losses: billing and checkout (45% of 1 to 2 star Trustpilot reviews), credits (27%), accuracy and consistency (39% of dislikes in happy G2 reviews), topology (20%), rigging on odd proportions (19%).
- Unprompted reviews average 3.2 stars, against 4.9 for invited ones. Buyers and AI answers can find the 3.2.
- 52% of developers in the GDC 2026 survey said generative AI is harming the industry.

## Acquisition plan (first 90 days)

1. **Paid social and search.** Social and video reach buyers in context; search catches intent ("3D models for Etsy", "hire a 3D artist"). Existing users excluded. $25 to 30K over 6 weeks.
2. **Certification-led acquisition and partner program.** The free certification is the reason to reach artists, shop owners and agencies, and graduates are nurtured into Studio seats or the partner tier. $10 to 15K over 6 to 8 weeks.
3. **Agentic outbound.** An AI agent named Myko researches each account, sends a tailored email with a genre sample and promotional credits, and invites prospects to send a sketch to turn into a model. 1,000 accounts over 4 weeks against a templated control.

## Launch goals

- Cost per paid business account under $700
- 500 certification enrollments and 200 certified in 8 weeks, with 20% or more at an identifiable business
- Agentic outbound positive reply rate at 2x the templated control
