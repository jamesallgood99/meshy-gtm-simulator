# Creative brief: Meshy SMB acquisition

*Working document for the Meshy Demand Gen take-home.*

## The ask

Messaging for the first 90 days of SMB acquisition: paid social and search ads, the certification landing page, and the first touch of Myko's outbound email. We want three distinct directions to test before we commit spend.

## Audience

Small game studios (10 to 200 people), solo commercial developers, freelance 3D artists, agent builders, product-visual startups, and print or e-commerce shops. Primary readers: the art director, the tech artist, the solo founder and the shop owner. The environment artist reads it too, and can kill a pilot.

## What we want them to think

"I stay in control of the art. Meshy takes the grind, and my work stays mine."

## Single-minded proposition

Base meshes in minutes, finished in your tools, and every model stays yours.

## Insight

Buyers pick Meshy for what it makes and how fast. They hesitate because of what AI means for their craft, their IP and their bill. Studios won't read the 4.7 rating; they'll read the one-star reviews an AI answer surfaces. Trust closes the sale.

## Message hierarchy

Capability wins the choice, ease removes the barrier, trust closes.

## Tone

Plain, specific and respectful of craft. Talk like a technical artist who has shipped games, not like an AI company. Confident, never hyped. No exclamation marks.

## Mandatories

- Never imply replacing artists, cutting headcount or beating a human modeler. Market the leash, not the genius.
- Promise a hybrid pipeline (base mesh in minutes, finished in your tools), never one-click final assets.
- Only use claims with proof in the MRD. The 1,331-artist blind test, user counts and the 4.7 rating stay out.
- Include at least one trust line in every direction: ownership, private by default, or opt out of training on any plan.
- Never disparage Tripo, Rodin or Hunyuan3D by name.

## Directions to explore

1. **Pipeline.** Lead with capability and speed inside the tools they already use, proven by the studio case studies. For the art director, tech artist and solo dev.
2. **Control.** Lead with trust: your models, your IP, your data, your call. For the studio head, the artist and anyone who has read a one-star review.
3. **Craft.** Lead with pride and the certification: you give it the signature, Meshy builds the form. For artists, freelancers and shop owners.
